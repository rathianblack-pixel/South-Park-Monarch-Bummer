Armor reveal and bonuses patch

Changed files: game.js, side-armor.js, side-armor.css, index.html.
No textures were changed in this patch; install over the previous side armor milestone patch, which contains the side armor PNGs.

Newly earned area armor and claimed side armor now open a centered reveal with a blurred backdrop, animated light and gentle armor sway, the set name, and a Continue button. Area armor appears after the battle reward is claimed.

Unique side armor bonuses apply while equipped:
- Choirguard Vestments: healing items and spells restore 2 extra HP.
- Forgeheart Plate: Guard reduces incoming damage by another 10%.
- Gilded Bargainer: combat coin rewards increase by 20% (rounded up).
- Roadkeeper's Cloak: reduces enemy signature ailment chance by 20% and incidental Bleeding chance from 50% to 40%.
- Village Defender: village raid attacks deal 10% less damage.
- Grave Lantern Keeper: Burning and Bleeding deal 1 less damage per turn (minimum 1).
- Maze Cartographer: attack accuracy gains 5 percentage points.
- Thronebreaker Regalia: damaging hits and Sword counters deal 2 extra damage to bosses.

Updated HTML asset versions to refresh cached game, side armor JS, and CSS.
